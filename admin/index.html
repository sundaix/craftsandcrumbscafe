Maintenance Tools

One-click, idempotent catalog fixes. Safe to run more than once — expand a tool to read what it does before running it.

Seed Starter Catalog

Pushes the built-in 57-item catalog into Firestore

If the live menu looks empty, the Firestore products collection hasn't been seeded yet. This pushes the built-in starter catalog (57 items) into Firestore — safe to click more than once.

Fix Drink Sizes (16/20/24oz)

Standardizes every drink onto three sizes, ₱20 apart

Rewrites every Coffee, Non-Coffee, and Tea product onto exactly three selectable sizes — 16oz, 20oz, and 24oz — each ₱20 apart, based on whatever that drink was already charging for its cheapest size. Use this if a drink's size selector on its product page looks wrong, is missing, or only shows one size instead of a real choice. Drinks already on 16oz/20oz/24oz are left untouched. Safe to click more than once.

Fill In Drink Customizations

Fills in missing sweetness, ice, milk, calories, nutrition

Too lazy to fill out every drink's customization options one by one? This scans your existing Coffee, Non-Coffee, and Tea products and fills in whatever's still missing — Sweetness Level and Ice Level option groups (plus Milk Type for anything with milk in it), calories, an about blurb, and a nutrition breakdown. It never touches a field a drink already has, so anything you've already customized is left exactly as-is. Safe to click more than once. Everything it adds can still be edited normally from each drink's Edit form afterward.

Clean Up Legacy Fields

Strips stray ingredients/allergens off non-food items

Some merch products (shirts, caps, bracelets, etc.) may still have leftover Ingredients/Allergens text saved on them from before those fields were limited to food items. This removes Ingredients and Allergens from every non-food product that has them — food items are left untouched. Safe to click more than once.

Flatten Per-Size Pricing

Collapses sized merch back to one flat price

Shirts, Caps, Shorts, or Socks products may still have old per-size pricing saved on them, where bigger sizes cost more than smaller ones. This rewrites every such product back to one flat price shared by all its sizes (the lowest price it was already charging). Safe to click more than once; products that are already flat are skipped.

Fix Broken Product Images

Replaces broken local file references with placeholders

Some products — mostly ones added before Cloudinary uploads existed — still have a bare local filename saved as their image (like "croissant.jpg" or "shirt1.png") instead of a real uploaded photo. Those never load on the live site and show up as a broken-image icon in the grid, the product page, and the Launch Popup. This swaps any broken reference for a clean "photo coming soon" placeholder — it never touches a product that already has a real uploaded photo. Safe to click more than once. Upload real photos for these any time from each product's Edit form.
