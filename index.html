Brewed to Perfection

Every cup starts with carefully sourced beans, roasted in small batches to bring out their fullest flavor. From the first pour to the last sip, we take our time to get it right — because great coffee isn't rushed, it's crafted.

Our story

From a class requirement to our first real café

Crafts & Crumbs started out as a group project in one of our college classes, just an assignment to design an online ordering system for a small coffee shop. We built it, presented it, and then kept working on it anyway because we liked where it was going.

That extra time turned the class project into a working online café, and the orders from friends and classmates convinced us to keep pushing. We're now putting that same plan into motion for our first physical café, so people can walk in and get the same coffee and pastries they've been ordering online.

Ordering

Browse the Menu or Merchandise page, add whatever you like to your cart, then head to checkout. You can pay on delivery or pickup, by card, or through GCash, GoTyme Bank, or Maribank.

Since we start preparing orders right away, changes or cancellations aren't guaranteed once an order is submitted. Call us or email cac.admin.info@gmail.com as soon as possible and we'll try our best to accommodate you.

Delivery & Pickup

Most orders are prepared within 15 to 20 minutes, then delivered depending on your distance from the store. Store pickup is usually faster since there's no travel time involved.

We currently deliver within Quezon City and nearby areas. If you're just outside our usual radius, call or email us and we'll let you know if we can still make it work.

Products
